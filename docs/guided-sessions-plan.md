# Guided sessions: rebuild plan (18 Nov 2026)

Founder, 18 Nov 2026: Breathe, Sit and Yoga Nidra are "not at all up to mark". Everything is weak: voice, instructions, pacing and feel. Rebuild them from the most authentic sources in the public domain. Start with one source and add more later. The end goal is a refined AI coach like Exercite: the camera watches the body and says whether a practice is done right, with explainer videos (Google's Fitbit Air and Health coach as a guiding light).

## The ten sources (public domain, or free official guidance)
1. **Hatha Yoga Pradipika**, tr. Pancham Sinh (1914), public domain. Its chapter 2 on pranayama covers Ujjayi, Bhastrika, Bhramari, Sheetali and Nadi shodhana; Kapalbhati is among its cleansing practices. **Start here.**
2. **Gheranda Samhita**, tr. S. C. Vasu (1914), public domain.
3. **Shiva Samhita**, tr. S. C. Vasu (1914), public domain.
4. **Raja Yoga** by Swami Vivekananda (1896), with the Yoga Sutras: public domain.
5. **Common Yoga Protocol**, Ministry of Ayush, Government of India: free official guidance with modern steps, counts and cautions. Rewrite it in our own words and credit it.
6. **MDNIY Y-Break protocol**, Government of India: free official guidance for short breaks.
7. **Anapanasati Sutta (MN 118)** and **Satipatthana Sutta (MN 10)**, tr. Bhikkhu Sujato: CC0, which means public domain. Use for Sit.
8. **Mandukya Upanishad**, tr. R. E. Hume (1921), public domain. The basis of Yoga Nidra.
9. **Bhagavad Gita, chapter 6 (Dhyana Yoga)**, in pre-1929 translations (Telang 1882, Arnold 1885): public domain.
10. **NIH NCCIH fact sheets** on yoga, meditation and breathing: US Government, public domain. Use for safety and evidence, with no medical claims.

## How each session is rebuilt
- **Instructions:** written from the sources, step by step, the way a teacher sits with you: posture, then the practice, then the count, then the rest, then a closing. Every line keeps a reference to its source.
- **Pacing:** exact timing from the texts and the Ayush protocol, for example Kapalbhati at about one stroke a second in rounds with natural rest, and Bhramari with a long, even hum. A breath circle on screen follows the same timing.
- **Voice and feel:** each session is made ahead as one finished audio track in Myra's calm voice, slow, with real pauses, over a soft tanpura or singing-bowl bed. It is not spoken line by line live. English and Hindi.
- **Check:** the founder listens to a preview and approves it before it goes live. The masters' rules apply: safe cautions, no medical claims.

## Phases
1. **Breathe, Myra's mix:** rebuilt from source 1 plus source 5. Preview MP3 for the founder.
2. **Sit** (Anapanasati, source 7) and **Yoga Nidra** (sources 8 and 10).
3. **Explainer videos:** one short clip per practice, made with an AI video tool or a filmed teacher, in the Fitbit Air / Google Health coach style.
4. **Camera coach**, like Exercite: the phone camera checks posture on the phone itself (spine, head, hands) and later the breath rhythm. This needs a new App Store build.
