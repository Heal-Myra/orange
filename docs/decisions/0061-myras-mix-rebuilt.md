# 0061 — Myra's mix rebuilt from the sources

Date: 18 Nov 2026 · Founder: guided sessions "not at all up to mark" (voice, instructions, pacing and feel); plan in
docs/guided-sessions-plan.md; script approved ("Myra mix scripts ok").

- **Sources:** Hatha Yoga Pradipika, chapter 2 (tr. Pancham Sinh, 1914, public domain) and the Ministry of Ayush Common
  Yoga Protocol (rewritten in our words, credited). Each step shows its source on screen.
- **Script (15 minutes):** Arrive (posture, jnana mudra, three slow breaths) → Bhastrika 3 × 15 full breaths, no
  breath-holding → Kapalbhati 3 × 30 strokes, about one a second → Anulom Vilom, in 4 out 6, about 11 rounds →
  Bhramari, in 4 hum 9, 10 rounds → three long Oms → Stillness → palms over the eyes. 10- and 5-minute versions have
  fewer rounds.
- **Feel:** Myra speaks only at the start and end of each round (and for the first two rounds of Anulom Vilom and
  Bhramari); a soft tick keeps the quick breaths; a chime marks each nostril switch; a tanpura plays underneath; the
  breath circle follows every breath. Sounds made in code (scripts/make-breath-sounds.py; nothing to license).
- **Where:** daytime Myra's mix (5 am to 8 pm). At night, on unwell days, with a care plan, and for single practices
  the earlier player runs until Sit, Yoga Nidra and the single practices are rebuilt the same way (plan, phase 2).
