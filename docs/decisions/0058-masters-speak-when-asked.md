# 0058 — Masters speak when asked, like a Guru

Date: 15 Nov 2026 · Founder

## Decision
Changes decision 0052. Myra stays the coordinator and the everyday voice, but the specialist masters (Food, Movement,
Sleep, the Mind monk, the Soul sage, Connectors) can speak to the person directly **when the person asks for one**.
Founder: "just like a Guru… they are available, but for day to day they send directions through their disciples or
assistant."

## How
- The person asks in Myra's chat or voice ("talk to the monk"), or taps the master in "Your team"; Myra may offer it.
- The master speaks in its own character and may go deeper; same Orange Book rules, care lines and one next step.
- "Back to Myra" hands back; the master's advice joins the team's notes so Myra never contradicts it.
- Morning huddle, nudges and weekly review still come from Myra alone.

## Why
People want the depth of the master sometimes, and one calm voice the rest of the time.
