# 0068 — Devices: the guru first, then its senses

Date: 26 Nov 2026 · Decided by: founder ("happy to go with your recommendations")

## Decision
Happy Orange will build devices, in stages, so the guru (Myra and the intelligence layer) is proven before money goes into hardware.
1. **Now:** the guru on devices people already own (phone, Apple Health, Oura or Watch, NeoSapien, food apps).
2. **Next:** a "Happy Orange edition" of a ready-made device from a partner factory (ring, band or clip-on), with our design and guru. Trigger: a few thousand daily users and a meaningful share paying (exact share to be set).
3. **Then:** our own device for what nobody captures well: what goes into the body. First pick: a small clip-on camera and microphone (food and drink seen, listening heard), paired with any ring or Watch. Glasses stay the long-term option through a partner that opens its camera to outside apps.

## Why
Devices complete the experience, give better data, keep people and support a membership; but a first wearable is costly and slow (rough estimate: crores of rupees and 18–30 months, to confirm with hardware founders), so the staged path learns hardware at low risk.

In the investor deck as the slide "Devices: when and how" (after Roadmap).
