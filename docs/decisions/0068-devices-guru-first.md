# 0068 — Devices: the guru first, then its senses

Date: 26 Nov 2026 · Decided by: founder ("happy to go with your recommendations")

## Decision
Happy Orange will build devices, in stages, so the guru (Myra and the intelligence layer) is proven before money goes into hardware.
1. **Now:** the guru on devices people already own (phone, Apple Health, Oura or Watch, NeoSapien, food apps).
2. **Next:** a "Happy Orange edition" of a ready-made device from a partner factory (ring, band or clip-on), with our design and guru. Trigger: a few thousand daily users and a meaningful share paying (exact share to be set).
3. **Then:** our own device for what nobody captures well: what goes into the body. **Ring + one thin "sense patch"** (founder, 26 Nov 2026): the thinnest camera and microphone in a patch the person attaches to whatever they like: glasses for people who wear them, otherwise a pendant, a brooch or any object. One module to make and certify; a light shows when it captures; short captures (meals, listening) rather than all-day filming, since the battery sets how thin it can be.
   **The ring is the trigger** (founder, 26 Nov 2026): its motion sensor spots the hand-to-mouth movement of eating at very low power and wakes the patch for a few photos of the plate; without a patch, it buzzes the phone ("Eating? Tap to snap"). A camera inside the ring was considered and set aside for now: the smallest battery, a lens that faces the palm or gets covered by food, and a thicker ring that reads the heart less well.

## Why
Devices complete the experience, give better data, keep people and support a membership; but a first wearable is costly and slow (rough estimate: crores of rupees and 18–30 months, to confirm with hardware founders), so the staged path learns hardware at low risk.
