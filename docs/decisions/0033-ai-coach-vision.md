Date: 25 October 2026 · Status: vision agreed (founder)

hand tracking (video never leaves the phone), compares with the practice data (`src/lib/practiceGuide.ts`, `checksFor`)

teacher-checked asanas → 5 personal coach. Safety: screen before strong practices, never push through pain, no medical
claims, rules reviewed by a certified teacher.

code from a skeleton of joints that matches the camera tracking's joints; AI image tools help choose the look. The
founder's animator friend (Rive rig, Happy Orange owns it) is the fallback.
