# 0009 Daily Wisdom from the person's own tradition

Date: 20 October 2026. Decided by: the founder.

## Decision
- The Soul tab's verse card is now **Daily Wisdom**: one passage a day from the tradition the person chooses.
- The choice is asked once after sign-up, right after the privacy screen. It is optional ("Prefer not to say" shows Universal wisdom) and can be changed any time in Profile → Daily Wisdom.
- Saved privately on the device and in the person's own account (Supabase auth user metadata), used only to pick the passage and to let Myra mention it. Deleting my data clears it.
- Meanings are explained by the AI in its own words, in English or Hindi, never by copying a published translation.

## Traditions and sources (all free to use)
| Tradition | Text | Source and licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hindu | Bhagavad Gita (Sanskrit + transliteration) | already bundled (see 0002) |
| Sikh | Japji Sahib, 39 parts (Gurmukhi) | Shabad OS database; Gurbani under Public Domain Mark 1.0. Transliteration generated by gurmukhi-utils (MIT); Shabad OS translations and transliterations (CC BY-SA) are not used |
| Buddhist | Dhammapada, all 423 verses (Pali + English) | SuttaCentral bilara-data, English by Bhikkhu Sujato, CC0 |
| Christian | 70 curated passages | Holy Bible, King James Version (public domain in India and the US), via es-kjv (MIT) |
| Muslim | 40 curated verses (Arabic + transliteration) | Arabic Uthmani text and Tanzil.net transliteration via quran-json (CC BY 4.0; attribution shown on the card). The packaged English translation is not used (copyright) |
| Universal | 141 sayings | Dhammapada (CC0) and King James Bible, alternating |
| Jain | Coming soon | No free-to-use source found yet; shows Universal wisdom meanwhile |

Arabic is drawn with the Amiri Quran font (SIL Open Font Licence) via @expo-google-fonts/amiri-quran, because phones' built-in Arabic fonts cannot draw the Qur'anic marks.

Rebuild the data with `node scripts/build-wisdom.js` (downloads listed in the script); output `assets/data/wisdom.json`.
